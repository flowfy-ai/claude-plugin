# Flowfy for Claude and ChatGPT

Flowfy connects your online store (Salla, Zid, Shopify, WooCommerce and custom sites) to your AI assistant as a read-only analyst. Ask what happened to your sales and ads, why it changed, what it cost you and what to do next. Every answer is grounded in your own Flowfy data: orders, ad spend, attribution, customer journeys and tracking health.

## What you get

- A connector to the Flowfy MCP server at `https://api.tryflowfy.com/mcp` with 24 read-only tools.
- Six decision skills: weekly optimization review, revenue drop investigation, cut or scale decision, budget reallocation plan, post-change review and tracking fix plan.

## Install

1. Add the plugin in Claude (Customize, Plugins) or ChatGPT (Plugins).
2. Connect Flowfy and sign in with your Flowfy account.
3. Choose the store the assistant may read, then approve.
4. Ask, for example: "What needs my attention in my store this week?"

## Your data

Flowfy only reads. It cannot change your store, ad accounts or Flowfy settings. The assistant sees data for the one store you approve, and you can disconnect it at any time from the assistant's connector settings. Uninstalling the Flowfy app from your store also ends the assistant's access.
